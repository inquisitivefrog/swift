# DinoGames: Design Principles for Young Children

**Audience:** Small children (e.g. ages 4–6) who **cannot read**.  
**Bias to avoid:** Grownup habits (relying on text, scanning quickly). Design must be **audio-first and visual**.

---

## 1. Clear visuals

- Use **large, recognizable images** (dinosaurs, eggs, trays, etc.).
- Prefer **images over text** for choices and feedback.
- Avoid depending on labels or instructions that only appear as text.
- Icons and pictures should be **immediately understandable** without reading.

---

## 2. Audio: Introduce each new game

- When a game is selected (e.g. from the game list), play an **intro clip** that names the game and sets the scene.
- Example: “Find Mama! A paleontologist found a lost egg…”
- File pattern: e.g. `Games/game-find-mama.m4a`, `Games/game-dino-lunch.m4a`.

---

## 3. Audio: Gameplay directions

- At the **start of play** (or start of each round), play a short clip that explains **what to do**.
- Example: “Help the paleontologist return the egg to its mother.”
- Players should understand the goal from **hearing**, not from reading.

---

## 4. Audio: How to succeed during play

- When the player is **in the middle of the game**, use audio to guide success:
  - Wrong choice: e.g. “Try again,” “Not that one.”
  - Right choice: e.g. “That’s right, you guessed it,” “Great match!”
- Avoid **text-only** feedback; pair with clear visuals and consistent audio.

---

## 5. Audio: Introduce each new object

- When a **new object** appears (e.g. a dinosaur, a lunch tray, a tooth), introduce it with **audio** (and optional short label).
- Example: tapping a teen dinosaur plays its name or a teen-intro clip.
- New items should be **heard first**, so the child can connect sound to image.

---

## Checklist for new or updated features

- [ ] Can a non-reader understand what to do from **images + audio**?
- [ ] Is there an **intro audio** for the game/screen?
- [ ] Are **directions** spoken, not only written?
- [ ] Is **success/failure** feedback spoken (and consistent)?
- [ ] Are **new objects** introduced with audio when they appear or when tapped?

---

*Keep these principles in mind when adding games, screens, or assets so the experience stays appropriate for young children.*
