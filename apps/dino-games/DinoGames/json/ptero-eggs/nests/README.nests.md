Pterosaur Clade Nests
---------------------
1. azhdarchid: decomposing vegetation mounds
2. ornithocheiroid: colonies on sandy beaches or lakeshores
3. specialists: inland wet mud or silt flats
4. transition: ground scrapes on solitary forest floor
5. basal: deep underground micro burrow

Pterosaur Egg Types
-------------------
1. massive clutch size: ornithocheiroid
2. single egg: transition
3. baby skeleton Pterodaustro: specialist
4. baby skeleton: azhdarchid 
5. two eggs: basal
