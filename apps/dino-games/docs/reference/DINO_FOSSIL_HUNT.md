Botany
------
**One story, two settings (land — not underwater):** paleobotany in **shale** and fossil search in **amber** among debris/overburden. Same quest teaches both; use **shale-splitter** for shale layers and a **dry** sieve stack to sort amber from matrix/debris. Do **not** use underwater/wet-sieve–only framing here; that belongs to the **Underwater** adventure.

1. discovery
   rock-color-chart, wide-brush, optical-lens,
   hand-held-sifting-screen, sifting-screen
2. excavate
   shale-splitter, dry-sieve-stack, dental-pick, fine-chisel, wide-brush
   *(shale-splitter = shale; dry-sieve-stack = amber in debris/overburden)*
3. preserve
   separation-layer, site-cover, specimen-crate, micro-vial-set
4. shipping-manifest, specimen-crate
